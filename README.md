## 📥 Install

Download the `.apk` that matches your phone's architecture from the [**Releases page**](https://github.com/naeem5877/vibedownloader-android/releases/latest).

| Device Architecture | Download | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 📱 **arm64-v8a** *(Recommended)* | [Download `.apk`](https://github.com/naeem5877/vibedownloader-android/releases/latest) | For virtually all modern smartphones (released in the last 6+ years) |
| 📱 **armeabi-v7a** | [Download `.apk`](https://github.com/naeem5877/vibedownloader-android/releases/latest) | For older or budget 32-bit Android devices |
| 🌐 **Universal** | [Download `.apk`](https://github.com/naeem5877/vibedownloader-android/releases/latest) | Works on all devices; contains all architectures in a single larger file |
| 💻 **x86_64 / x86** | [Download `.apk`](https://github.com/naeem5877/vibedownloader-android/releases/latest) | For Android emulators and Intel/AMD-powered tablets |

> **System Requirements:** Android 7.0 (Nougat, API 24) or newer.

### Step-by-Step Installation

1. Tap on the downloaded **`.apk`** file from your browser downloads or file manager.
2. If prompted, enable **"Allow from this source"** or **"Install unknown apps"** in Android Settings.
3. Tap **Install** and wait for the package installer to finish.
4. Launch **VibeDownloader** from your app drawer.

> **Why is VibeDownloader not on Google Play Store?**<br />
> Google Play strictly bans YouTube and social video downloaders. Distributing via GitHub Releases ensures that no core features are stripped, downloads remain 100% ad-free, and code remains open source for anyone to audit.

## 🛟 Help & Setup

<a id="cookies"></a>

### 🍪 Cookies (Optional)

Some content requires authentication, such as age-restricted videos or private Instagram accounts. You can configure cookies in the app settings:

- Cookies are stored **exclusively on your device** and are only used to authenticate your requested downloads.
- They are never uploaded or sent to external servers.
- **Tip:** For best results, log in using a private/incognito browser window, export cookies, and close the session immediately to prevent cookie rotation.

<a id="troubleshooting"></a>

### 🧰 Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary><b>A download fails or only lower qualities (e.g. 360p) are shown</b></summary>

<br />

1. Open **Settings** and ensure the engine (`yt-dlp`) is updated to the latest build.
2. Toggle off VPNs or proxies. Many public VPN servers are throttled or flagged by YouTube.
3. Try again with cookies configured if the content is age-restricted or private.
4. If the issue persists, open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/naeem5877/vibedownloader-android/issues) with the public link and device model.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Downloads pause or fail when the screen turns off</b></summary>

<br />

Some Android manufacturers (Xiaomi, Samsung, Huawei, etc.) aggressively kill background processes to save battery. To ensure large downloads finish uninterrupted:

1. Go to your phone's **Settings → Apps → VibeDownloader**.
2. Tap **Battery / Battery Saver** and select **Unrestricted** or **No Restrictions**.
3. If your system has an **Autostart** or **Background execution** toggle, enable it for VibeDownloader.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>"App not installed" or architecture error when installing</b></summary>

<br />

Ensure you download the APK matching your device's architecture:
- Modern devices (past 6 years): **`arm64-v8a`**
- If you are unsure which CPU your phone has, download the **`universal`** APK which works on any Android device.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>"ffmpeg cannot start on this device"</b></summary>

<br />

This occurred on older v2.0.0 builds on devices utilizing 16 KB memory pages. Update to **v2.2.0 or newer**. The fix first shipped in v2.1.0, and v2.2.0 includes it.

</details>

### ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is VibeDownloader free and safe?</b></summary>

<br />

Yes, 100% free, ad-free, and open source under the **GNU GPL v3.0** license. You can inspect every line of source code. Downloads and cookies stay on your device.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are downloaded files saved on Android?</b></summary>

<br />

- **Videos & Images:** Saved to your device's `Movies/VibeDownloader` or `DCIM` and immediately indexed into your system **Gallery**.
- **Music & Audio:** Saved to `Music/VibeDownloader` and indexed into your system music player.
- You can also view, play, share, or delete any file directly in the in-app **Library**.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I download only the audio or MP3 from a video?</b></summary>

<br />

Yes! Whenever you fetch a video, VibeDownloader displays an **Audio Only** section offering Best Quality MP3 (320kbps), Standard MP3 (192kbps), and uncompressed WAV.

</details>

<details>

### Tech Stack Details

- **Framework:** React Native 0.83.1 with TypeScript & React 19
- **Native Engine:** `io.github.junkfood02.youtubedl-android` bundling CPython and `yt-dlp`
- **JS Solver:** QuickJS native engine for resolving InnerTube challenges locally
- **Media Processing:** Bundled `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` binaries with 16 KB page alignment
- **Storage:** Android MediaStore API integration for seamless Gallery recognition
- **Error Tracking:** `@sentry/react-native` with automated PII & URL redaction

### How Downloads Work

1. **Resolution:** Pasted URLs first query YouTube InnerTube player endpoints directly for instantaneous format, subtitle, and audio track discovery.
2. **Fallback:** If direct resolution fails or for complex platforms, the app transparently delegates to the embedded `yt-dlp` engine.
3. **Execution:** The native Kotlin module spins up a background thread on the CPython runtime, passing flags to stream and post-process via `ffmpeg`.
4. **Gallery Insertion:** Completed files are committed to the public Android MediaStore (`Movies` or `Music`), making them immediately accessible across other mobile apps.
